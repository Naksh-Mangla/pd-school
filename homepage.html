Guiding Principles

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

It is our endeavour to mold our students to be compassionate, progressive and professionally successful human being responsive to global culture and heritage in an environment of sharing and caring. We believe that every child has a talent, which can be enhanced if fostered in a conductive environment. We strive to provide a lively, warm and purpose ful atmosphere.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a vibrant forum for  quality education, with a scientific temper, adaptive to the changing needs of time, to ensure all round development of the child and expose him/her to a wide spectrum of co-cultural learning which is essential to make the child confident and ready to step out. At same time acquaint them with the Indian culture and  spiritual heritage.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get in touch with my child's teacher?

 You can contact your child’s teacher through scheduling a meeting with them during their office hours. Additionally, the school may have a communication platform or app for parents to stay updated on their child’s progress.

Is there transportation available?

 Yes, the school provides transportation services for students. Please contact the school administration for more information about routes and fees.

Are there any extracurricular activities offered?

 Yes, the school offers a variety of extracurricular activities including sports, arts, music, and clubs. Students are encouraged to explore their interests outside of the classroom.

What is the school's approach to discipline?

 The school promotes a positive behavior management system that emphasizes respect, responsibility, and fairness. Discipline policies and procedures are outlined in the student handbook, and parents are encouraged to reinforce these values at home.

How does the school handle emergencies or safety concerns?

 The safety and well-being of students are a top priority. The school has emergency procedures in place and conducts regular drills to ensure everyone is prepared. In case of an emergency, parents will be notified immediately through the school’s communication channels.
